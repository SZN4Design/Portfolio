THE IDEA

More data is only useful
when it helps you act.

Market Demand Forecaster, also called Demand Flow Advisor, explores how dealers could make inventory decisions with more context and less guesswork.

The concept combines city- and model-level demand, ranked inventory recommendations, confidence cues and an explanation of the signals behind each suggestion. Forecasts and dashboard values are mock data used to demonstrate the intended experience.

CONCEPT STUDY / ILLUSTRATIVE DATAThe screenshots and live prototype use mock data. Scores, forecasts, percentages and performance figures illustrate the design; they are not measured business results. Benefits described in the boards are proposed outcomes.
