THE IDEA

Turn a listing task
into a coaching moment.

ListIQ explores how an automotive marketplace can guide dealers toward more complete, useful listings. Photos, descriptions, pricing and missing details become concrete, prioritized actions.

A quality score provides a starting point, while plain-language recommendations explain why each change matters. The intended workflow keeps guidance inside the listing editor and lets dealers preview the effect of proposed improvements.

CONCEPT STUDY / ILLUSTRATIVE DATAThe screenshots and live prototype use mock data. Scores, forecasts, percentages and performance figures illustrate the design; they are not measured business results. Benefits described in the boards are proposed outcomes.
