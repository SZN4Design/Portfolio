ABOUT / SZN4

Hi, I’m Sabrina.

Designer. Observer. Professional overthinker of why people do the things they do.

I’m a Toronto-based designer with a background somewhere between design, technology, ecommerce and human behaviour.

I love making digital experiences look beautiful, obviously — but I’m much more interested in understanding why something works.

SCROLL INTO MY BRAIN ↓

01 / CURIOSITY

I’m always asking why.

Why did someone click that button?

Why did they scroll past that section?

Why does one experience feel effortless while another makes you want to close the tab immediately?

That curiosity is usually where my design process starts.

03 / AWAY FROM THE SCREEN

Some of my best ideas happen when I’m nowhere near my laptop.

You’ll probably find me somewhere between a yoga mat, a body of water and a sketchbook.

MOVEMENT

Getting out of my head.

Yoga and movement pull me out of perfect-everything mode and back into being present. That usually gives curiosity a little more room.

WATER

Where everything gets quieter.

I love being near water. It gives my brain enough space to stop forcing an answer — which is usually when the better one shows up.

MAKING

Paper before pixels.

I sketch jewellery, shapes and ideas simply because I need to see what they could become. Not everything has to start perfectly — sometimes it just has to start.

04 / IT ALL COMES BACK TO CURIOSITY

Movement teaches me to notice.

Water gives me space to think.

Sketching reminds me to explore.

And I think all of that makes me a better designer.
