THE IDEA

Which car is right
for my life?

Car research can mean moving between reviews, dealership listings, finance calculators and conflicting opinions. CRVNCHMODE explores how those pieces could become a clearer, buyer-first journey.

The Smart Buyer Quiz starts with budget and everyday needs. An explainable CrunchScore brings financial reality, reliability, lifestyle fit, trust and resale strength into the recommendation, so the shopper can understand the reasoning.

CONCEPT STUDY / ILLUSTRATIVE DATAThe screenshots and live prototype use mock data. Scores, forecasts, percentages and performance figures illustrate the design; they are not measured business results. Benefits described in the boards are proposed outcomes.
